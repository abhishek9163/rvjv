# Dashboard Tabbed Interface

Based on your request, I have completely redesigned the Dashboard to feature a modern, Tabbed Interface. This organizes all your information neatly and removes the need for endless scrolling.

## New Tab Structure

When you visit your dashboard, you will now see three sleek tabs at the top:

1. **Overview Tab (Default)**
   - Displays all your top-level metric cards.
   - For Managers: Total Employees, Total Vehicles, Total Modules.
   - For DEOs: Total Entered, Verified, Pending, Rejected, and Modules Assigned.
   - This keeps the main dashboard view focused solely on high-level analytics.

2. **My Modules Tab**
   - Click this to view the grid of all your assigned dynamic modules.
   - Managers will also find their "Archived Modules (Trash)" section at the bottom of this tab.
   - This separates module management from the analytical data.

3. **Inventory Tracker Tab**
   - This tab contains the "Select Inventory Modules to View" checkboxes.
   - It houses all the detailed Inventory Overview panels and statistics.
   - By isolating this, you can now toggle and manage your inventory views without cluttering your main dashboard!

## How it works
- The tabs are extremely fast. Clicking between them instantly switches the view **without reloading the page**.
- The active tab is highlighted with your theme's primary color so you always know where you are.
